Our Story

Seventeen years of island recipes, clay pots and warm Colombo hospitality.

Who we are

Authentic Sri Lankan,
cooked the slow way

Saffron & Clay is a family-run Sri Lankan restaurant serving traditional rice & curry, kottu, hoppers and coastal seafood. We cook in clay pots over open flame, roast our own curry powders and source vegetables from farms in Nuwara Eliya and Dambulla.

Whether you dine with us on Flower Road or order online, every plate is made to share — the way Sri Lankan food is meant to be eaten.

2009Established

Our Mission

To serve honest, home-style Sri Lankan food made from scratch, at fair prices, with hospitality that makes every guest feel like family.

Our Vision

To be Colombo's most trusted Sri Lankan kitchen and share the island's regional flavours — from Jaffna to Galle — with the world.

What makes us special

In-house spice blends, clay-pot cooking, local farm produce and recipes passed down three generations of our family.
